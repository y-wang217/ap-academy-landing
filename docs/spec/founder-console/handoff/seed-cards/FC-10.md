## Outcome
Harden the finished bridge, prove it is both beautiful and truthful, and prepare it for a founder-controlled launch.

## Scope
- Compare browser captures with reference aesthetic at prescribed resolutions
- Test all paneled states, keyboard, zoom, screen readers, reduced motion and latency
- Verify end-to-end sales, media and operations flows with sanitized realistic fixtures
- Record final evidence and founder-only release gate, no auto-release

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Screenshots at 1920×1080 and 1440×900 show required visual signature; 1280×720 and 390×844 remain usable.
- AC2: All controls and gauges work with keyboard, announce label/status, and honor reduced-motion.
- AC3: No demo contact names, financial metrics, fake status or hardcoded illustrative date appear in deployed production.
- AC4: Founder-only API access holds under direct route probing; integration failures degrade safely.
- AC5: At least one end-to-end safe record loop succeeds and is supported by audit/readback evidence.
- AC6: Readiness explicitly states whether deployed, verified and founder-approved, not just PR merged.

## Required test coverage
- T1: Screenshot side-by-side review at 4 sizes.
- T2: Automated E2E and auth/RLS regression pack.
- T3: Manual founder dry-run checklist and release receipt.

## Explicit non-goals
- No auto-launch, no new scope or feature backlog expansion in polish card.
