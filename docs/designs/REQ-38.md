# REQ-38: People sync, local copies of staff and students that the scheduler and parent views read

Card: https://app.notion.com/p/3fbd6ae73dd7818ba1faccf5f76aca96 (Feedback 9, Risk High, Priority P1). Copied from the approved design on the card.

**Summary:** The tracker copies staff names, subjects and active state, and student display names and stage, from Notion into new `tracker.staff` and `tracker.student_people` tables every hour and when the owner taps Sync now. Every scheduler, parent-calendar and daily-task screen then reads those local rows under RLS, shows "Names last updated N hours ago" when the copy is stale, and a teacher whose local staff row is inactive loses scope on the next request.

**Risk:** High. It changes what a signed-in teacher can see and do (teacher scope now comes from the local staff row, and student names are limited by RLS to that teacher's series), it stores staff and student names in Postgres for the first time (a new destination for personal data under ADR 0004), and the sync rewrites and archives existing rows.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: (tables) `tracker.staff` and `tracker.student_people` exist with exactly the columns in the Migration section. Neither table has a parent, email, phone, rate, notes or billing column. Migration adds no rows.
- AC2: (RLS) owner reads all rows in their org. A scheduling teacher (live `staff_links` row) reads every active, non-archived staff row in the org plus their own row, and only the `student_people` rows whose `notion_student_id` is on a `class_series` row with their `notion_staff_id`. A parent reads only their published linked child's `student_people` row and only the `staff` rows of teachers on that child's series. A student, a membership teacher with no staff link, and `anon` read nothing. Cross-org reads return zero rows. `authenticated` has no insert, update or delete on either table, nor on `tracker.sync_runs`.
- AC3: (Notion reads) the sync reads Staff with `filter_properties` for Name, Status, Function and Subjects only (never Email) and Students & Parents with `filter_properties` for Student Name and Stage only, over every stage, with full pagination.
- AC4: (apply) `tracker.apply_people_sync` upserts by `(org_id, notion id)` only. A rename updates the same row. A row missing from the Notion read gets `archived_at` set and `active = false` and is never deleted. Staff `active` is `isActiveTeachingStaff` computed at sync. Student `active` means stage Active or Onboarding. A new row is inserted only when it is active, or when the org already references its id (`class_series`, `staff_links` for staff; `class_series`, `students.notion_student_id` for students). A row that comes back reappears with `archived_at = null`.
- AC5: (run record) every sync attempt writes one `tracker.sync_runs` row with `started_at`, `finished_at`, `trigger` (`schedule` or `owner`), `result` (`ok` or `failed`), four counts, and on failure an `error` of at most 300 characters that holds no Notion payload.
- AC6: (failed sync keeps rows) a Notion error, a parse failure, an empty Staff or Students read while active rows exist (`empty_read`), or an invalid payload records a `failed` run and changes no `staff` or `student_people` row.
- AC7: (schedule) every authorised call to `POST /tracker/api/scheduler/daily-task/run` runs the people sync for `DAILY_TASK_ORG_ID` with the service client before the `isDue` check. A failed sync does not stop the daily task. The body gains `people: "ok" | "failed" | "not_configured"`. Status codes are unchanged.
- AC8: (on demand) `POST /tracker/api/scheduler/people/sync` runs the sync for the owner's org through the owner's own client. Teacher 403, signed out 401, cross-site 403. A second call within `TUNING.peopleSyncCooldownSeconds` (60) of the last run's start returns 429 `too_soon` and does not call Notion. `GET` returns `{ lastOk, lastFailure }` for the owner only.
- AC9: (reads are local) `fetchStaff` and `studentNames` in `lib/scheduler/deps.ts`, identity, the week views, availability, conflicts, the import screen's teacher names, the daily task (owner path and run route), the summary sync, the parent calendar and the owner's Notion link picker read the local rows. `lib/notion/cached.ts` no longer exports `getStaffList` or `getStudentNames`. No file under `lib/scheduler/`, `lib/data/`, `app/` or `components/` imports `fetchStaff` or `fetchStudentNames` from `lib/notion/`, except `lib/scheduler/people-sync-wiring.ts`.
- AC10: (live reads that stay) Weekly Schedule import (`fetchScheduledStudents`, `fetchStudentSchedule`), the summary write (`writeWeeklySchedule`) and billing (`lib/notion/billing.ts`) still read Notion live. The new tracker ADR lists them.
- AC11: (freshness) `peopleFreshness(lastOk, now)` returns null when the last good sync is newer than `TUNING.peopleStaleMinutes` (120), "Names last updated N hours ago" from 120 minutes to under 48 hours, "Names last updated N days ago" from 48 hours, and "Names have not been loaded yet." when there has never been a good sync. Each AC12 page shows that notice above its content and still renders its data.
- AC12: (where the notice shows) `/schedule`, `/schedule/availability`, `/schedule/conflicts`, `/scheduler/schedules`, `/parent/[studentId]/calendar`, and the owner's parent panel on `/students/[studentId]`.
- AC13: (revocation) `resolveSchedulerIdentity` gives `none` to a linked teacher whose local staff row is inactive, archived or missing, even when the `staff_links` row is not revoked. It gives `unavailable` when there has never been a good sync or the local read fails. An owner with a link to an inactive row stays `admin` with `staff: null`.
- AC14: (pickers) inactive or archived students are not in the owner's student picker on `/schedule` or the Notion link picker. Inactive or archived staff are not in teacher pickers. An existing series with an archived student or teacher still shows that name.
- AC15: (owner status) on `/schedule` the owner sees "Names synced N minutes ago." or "Last sync failed at HH:MM. Names are from HH:MM." and a Sync now button with a pending state. A failed tap shows an error and never claims success.
- AC16: (admin email) `toAdminStaff` takes `email` from the live `tracker.staff_links` row, or null. No email is copied from Notion.
- AC17: (docs) a tracker ADR records the tables, the RPC, the cadence, which reads are local and which stay live, and the rollout step. `scheduler-operations.md` gains a "Names and the people sync" section. No UI string or doc added by this card has an em-dash.

## Migration

- File: `supabase/migrations/0022_people_sync.sql`, applied as `0022_people_sync`. Check: `supabase/tests/people_sync.sql`. No `drop`, `delete`, `truncate` or `alter ... drop`, so `apply_migration` can apply it.
- `tracker.staff`: `org_id uuid not null references tracker.orgs on delete restrict`, `notion_staff_id uuid not null`, `name text not null check (char_length(name) between 1 and 200)`, `subjects text[] not null default '{}'`, `active boolean not null`, `synced_at timestamptz not null`, `archived_at timestamptz`, `created_at`, `updated_at`. Primary key `(org_id, notion_staff_id)`. Index on `(org_id) where active and archived_at is null`.
- `tracker.student_people`: `org_id`, `notion_student_id uuid not null`, `name text not null check (char_length(name) between 1 and 200)`, `stage text not null check (char_length(stage) <= 60)`, `active boolean not null`, `synced_at`, `archived_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`. Primary key `(org_id, notion_student_id)`.
- `tracker.sync_runs`: `id uuid pk`, `org_id`, `kind text check (kind in ('people'))`, `trigger text check (trigger in ('schedule','owner'))`, `actor uuid references auth.users on delete set null`, `started_at`, `finished_at`, `result text check (result in ('ok','failed'))`, `staff_upserted`, `staff_archived`, `students_upserted`, `students_archived` (integers, default 0, non-negative), `error text check (error is null or char_length(error) <= 300)`. Index `(org_id, kind, started_at desc)`.
- `set_updated_at` triggers on `staff` and `student_people`. Audit on insert, and on update only `when (old.name, old.active, old.archived_at) is distinct from (new.name, new.active, new.archived_at)`, so hourly `synced_at` touches never flood `audit_log`. No audit on `sync_runs`.
- Helpers, security definer, `set search_path = ''`: `tracker.is_my_series_student(target_org, target_student)` (a `class_series` row in that org with that student and `notion_staff_id = tracker.my_staff_id(target_org)`); `tracker.is_my_childs_teacher(target_org, target_staff)` (a `class_series` row with that staff id where `tracker.is_my_childs_schedule(org_id, notion_student_id)`).
- RLS select policies only (no insert, update or delete policies). `staff`: `is_org_owner(org_id) or (my_staff_id(org_id) is not null and ((active and archived_at is null) or notion_staff_id = my_staff_id(org_id))) or is_my_childs_teacher(org_id, notion_staff_id)`. `student_people`: `is_org_owner(org_id) or is_my_series_student(org_id, notion_student_id) or is_my_childs_schedule(org_id, notion_student_id)`. `sync_runs`: `is_org_owner(org_id)`.
- `tracker.apply_people_sync(p_org uuid, p_trigger text, p_started_at timestamptz, p_staff jsonb, p_students jsonb, p_error text) returns tracker.sync_runs`, security definer. Raises `insufficient_privilege` unless `tracker.is_org_owner(p_org)` or `auth.role() = 'service_role'`. Takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on the org so two syncs serialise. With `p_error`, an invalid payload, or an empty array while that table has active rows, inserts a `failed` run and returns. Otherwise, in one transaction: updates existing rows by key, inserts new rows by the AC4 rule, archives rows not in the payload, inserts the `ok` run with counts.
- `tracker.people_synced_at(target_org uuid) returns timestamptz`, security definer, stable: the latest `ok` people run's `finished_at`, returned only to the org owner, a caller with a live staff link in that org, or a parent with a claimed link in that org; null for anyone else.
- Grants: `revoke all` on the three tables from `anon, authenticated`; `grant select` to `authenticated`; `grant all` to `service_role`. Functions: `revoke all from public, anon`; `grant execute` on `apply_people_sync` to `authenticated, service_role` and on `people_synced_at` to `authenticated`.
- `supabase/tests/people_sync.sql` cast: owner O1, scheduling teacher T1 (live link, one series with student S1), teacher T2 (live link, no series), membership teacher M1, parent P1 (claimed link to a published child with `notion_student_id` = S1), student U1, and owner O2 of Org Two. It proves: the exact column sets (no parent, email, phone, rate, notes or billing column); O1 reads all rows; T1 reads active staff plus own row and only S1; T2 reads no `student_people`; P1 reads only S1 and only T1's staff row; M1, U1, `anon` and O2 read zero rows of Org One; `authenticated` insert, update and delete on all three tables fail with 42501; `apply_people_sync` as T1 or P1 raises 42501 and as O1 works; rename keeps the row; a missing id is archived, not deleted; an inactive unreferenced id is not inserted; a referenced inactive id is inserted; an empty staff array with active rows records `failed` and leaves rows unchanged; `p_error` records `failed` and leaves rows unchanged; `people_synced_at` returns the time to O1, T1, P1 and null to U1 and O2; an unchanged re-sync adds no `audit_log` row.
- Ships as its own PR first (`req/REQ-38-migration`, human-merged and applied). Applying it changes nothing for users: no code reads the tables until the feature PR deploys.

## Risks and edge cases

Copied from the card.

- **First deploy, empty tables.** Until the first good sync, teachers get `unavailable` and names say "not loaded yet". Live has 0 active staff links and 0 series today, so nobody is affected. Rollout step: right after the feature deploys, the owner taps Sync now on `/schedule` (or runs the Daily teacher texts workflow by hand).
- **Notion returns an empty or partial list** (lost share, renamed database). The empty-read guard records `failed` instead of archiving everyone. A renamed property makes `propertyIds` throw, which is also a `failed` run.
- **Two syncs at once** (cron and Sync now). The advisory lock serialises them; the second applies the same data. The cooldown stops repeated taps from hitting Notion.
- **Owner can call `apply_people_sync` with any payload.** The owner already controls staff links and series for their org, and the function only touches that org. Accepted.
- **RLS lag for a deactivated teacher.** `identity.ts` cuts app scope on the next request after the sync. Raw PostgREST reads through `my_staff_id()` still follow `staff_links` only, as today; the immediate cut stays revoking the link (ADR 0004). See Decision 7.
- **A churned student never seen before** is not copied (AC4 insert rule), which keeps the copy to people the tracker uses.
- **Teacher student names narrow.** A teacher now sees names only for students on their own series. This matches the Feedback 5 rule and only shrinks what teachers see.
- **Audit volume.** Hourly syncs that change nothing write no audit rows (conditional trigger), proven in the RLS test.

## Approach, test plan, decisions

As on the card: Notion readers `fetchStaffForSync` and `fetchStudentPeople`; pure `runPeopleSync` in `lib/scheduler/people-sync.ts`; wiring in `lib/scheduler/people-sync-wiring.ts`; the people store in `lib/data/people.ts` (`toStaffRecord` maps `active` to the Status and Function values `isActiveTeachingStaff` expects, Decision 5); identity reads `getStaff` and `syncedAt`; hourly sync inside `handleDailyTaskRun` (Decision 8); Sync now on `/schedule` (Decision 12); `getStaffList` and `getStudentNames` removed from `cached.ts` (Decision 13); no greeting name column (Decision 3); separate `student_people` table (Decision 4); one definer RPC as the only writer (Decision 6); `my_staff_id()` unchanged (Decision 7); parents read only their child's teachers' staff rows (Decision 10).
