# REQ-25 Parent 4/4: Billing tab, this month's invoice, credits and past invoices

Copied from the approved card (Risk High: the design-approver returned Needs Charlie, and Charlie moved the card to Approved by hand). The card is the source; this copy sits next to the diff for review.

**Summary:** The parent Billing tab at `/tracker/parent/[studentId]/billing` shows, read-only, the child's invoice for this month, their missed-session credits, their past invoices (each opening its PDF through a no-store server proxy) and the rate line. The data is read from the Notion Invoices, Missed Sessions & Credits and Students & Parents databases. Each read is filtered on the child's own Students & Parents page, and only the listed properties are requested.

**Risk:** High. A parent's family billing data (amounts, credits, invoice PDFs, a sibling's first name) moves from Notion to the parent's browser, which is a new destination. The gate is app code (the Notion `Student` relation matched against `students.notion_student_id`), not RLS, because Notion has no row security for the tracker's token.

## Problem
A parent wants to see on a phone what they owe this month, which missed sessions are credited and where, and their past invoices. REQ-22 shipped the shell with a placeholder ("Invoices will show here.") in `apps/tracker/app/parent/[studentId]/billing/page.tsx`. This card fills that tab. The data lives in Notion, not Postgres: **Invoices** (`be0f91e4-f054-4055-9ca6-c4e1a22c4f27`), **Missed Sessions & Credits** (`38cdcded-9bef-4e68-bceb-a56920f098bd`) and **Students & Parents** (`fe3d651a-70c6-4d1e-8a38-656163d6bc63`). The mock is `apps/tracker/docs/spec/parent-views/billing-375.webp` (variant C). Correction 2 in `apps/tracker/docs/spec/parent-views.md` applies: an Open credit nets off the next invoice.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: `invoiceStatus(invoice, today)` (pure) returns `Paid` for Status Paid. For Status Sent it returns `Due` when Due is empty or on or after `today`, and `Overdue` when Due is before `today`. `today` is the America/Toronto date. Draft and Void never reach it (AC3).
- AC2: Netting. `creditsOnInvoice(invoice, credits)` returns only credits with Credit Status `Applied` whose `Applied To` names this invoice's number (the text before the first comma, trimmed) or whose page ID is in the invoice's `Credits` relation. An `Open` credit is never on any invoice's "Credit applied" line, even if its Billed Month equals the invoice's Month or it sits in the relation. `openTotal(credits)` sums Hours × Rate (CAD) over `Open` rows only.
- AC3: Draft and Void invoices never reach the page. The Notion query excludes them, and the parser drops any row with those statuses or whose `Student` relation does not contain the child's `notion_student_id`.
- AC4: "This month" is the visible invoice whose Month equals the current Toronto `YYYY-MM`. If two rows match, it is the one with the latest Issued date. The card shows `<Month name> invoice`, the Amount (CAD) as the big number, Issued, Due and the status pill (Paid ok, Due warn, Overdue error). When the invoice's Credit Applied (CAD) is above 0, it adds `Credit applied: -$N` with one line per linked credit, `Missed <Mon D> · <course>`. No match shows "No invoice yet this month".
- AC5: Credits lists every credit for the child, Date descending, with date, course, hours (`1.0h`) and amount (Hours × Rate, omitted when either is empty). Each row has a pill: Open warn, Applied ok, Made up ok, Forfeited error. The header shows "Open total" and the summed amount. When any row is Open, a footnote reads "Open credits come off your next invoice." No rows shows "No credits".
- AC6: Past invoices lists every visible invoice except the one on the card, Month descending then Issued descending, at most 24. Each row shows `Mon YYYY`, the amount and the status pill. A row whose invoice has a PDF is a link to the PDF route (AC9); a row without one is plain text. No rows shows "No past invoices".
- AC7: Shared invoices. When another visible Invoices row has the same Invoice number, and that row's student has the same non-empty Billing Family as the child (trimmed, case-insensitive), the child's row shows "Shared invoice with \<sibling first name\>" under its amount. This applies on the card and on past rows. The amount is always the child's own row and siblings are never summed. A same-number row with another or empty Billing Family shows nothing. If the sibling lookup fails, the line is left out and the page still renders.
- AC8: The rate line at the bottom reads `Rate: $60/h · 2x/week`, from `Hourly Rate (CAD)` and `Lesson Cadence` on the child's Students & Parents page. A missing part is left out. Both missing leaves out the line.
- AC9: `GET /tracker/parent/[studentId]/billing/invoices/[invoiceId]` streams the PDF with `Content-Type: application/pdf`, `Content-Disposition: inline; filename="<invoice number>.pdf"`, `Cache-Control: private, no-store, max-age=0`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`. The Notion file URL never reaches the browser, is never stored, and is never logged.
- AC10: The PDF route returns 404 with no body detail when the caller is not a parent; `studentId` is not one of the caller's published children, or that child has a null `notion_student_id`; either ID is not a GUID; the page's parent is not the Invoices data source; its `Student` relation does not contain the child's `notion_student_id`; its Status is not Sent or Paid; it has no Notion-hosted file; or the file host is not allowlisted (Decision 6). It returns 502 when the file fetch fails, redirects or is over 10 MB.
- AC11: Each Notion request on this path asks only for the listed properties via `filter_properties` (property IDs). Invoices: Invoice, Month, Amount (CAD), Credit Applied (CAD), Credits, Issued, Due, Status, PDF, Student. Credits: Date, Course, Hours, Rate (CAD), Credit Status, Applied To, Student. The child's Students & Parents page: Hourly Rate (CAD), Lesson Cadence, Billing Family. A sibling's page: Student Name, Billing Family. The sharer lookup: Invoice, Student, Status. Both list queries filter on `Student` `relation.contains` the child's `notion_student_id`. Unknown properties are dropped at the Zod parse, so a canary `Parent Phone` or `Notes` value returned by Notion never reaches the HTML.
- AC12: The billing files never name `Parent Phone`, `Parent Email`, `Parent Name`, `Sales Notes`, `Notes`, `Discounts`, `Last Invoice`, `Lessons`, `Payouts`, `Reason` or `Billed Month`, the Lessons data source `6300f58a` or `unstable_cache`. They make no `.insert(`, `.update(`, `.upsert(`, `.delete(` or `.rpc(` call, use no service-role client, send no `PATCH` or `POST /v1/pages` to Notion, and do not use `console.`. This is a source scan. The view model type has no `url` field.
- AC13: A child whose `notion_student_id` is null, or a server without `NOTION_TOKEN`, shows "Billing not available yet" and makes no Notion request. A failed read of invoices, credits or the child's page shows "Could not load billing. Try again later." and no cards. Empty states are never shown in place of an error.
- AC14: A parent sees only their linked child's invoices and credits. Another family's amounts and courses never appear, and the PDF route returns 404 for another family's invoice ID (e2e).
- AC15: The tab has no button other than Sign out, no input, textarea or select, no payment or e-transfer text, and no horizontal scroll at 375px. The copy has no em-dashes.
- AC16: Tracker ADR 0048 (or the next free number at build time; REQ-24 takes 0047) covers the Notion read path and its app-side gate, the allowed properties, the PDF proxy, no caching and the allowlisted file hosts, the Overdue rule and the netting display rule, and the sibling first name. It is listed in `apps/tracker/docs/decisions/README.md`.

## Approach
- **Pure domain** `apps/tracker/lib/domain/billing.ts`: `invoiceStatus`, `creditsOnInvoice`, `openTotal`, `appliedToNumber`, `creditAmount` (cents), `splitInvoices` (`{ current, past }`, the 24 cap) and `sharedWith`. No React, Next, Supabase, Notion or clock.
- **Notion adapter** `apps/tracker/lib/notion/billing.ts` (`server-only`): plain fetch and Zod in the `students.ts` pattern, read-only. `fetchChildBilling`, `fetchInvoices` (Student contains, Status ≠ Draft, ≠ Void, Month descending, paging guard; PDF parsed to `hasPdf`), `fetchCredits`, `fetchInvoiceSharers` (one `or` of `Invoice title equals` over up to 25 numbers), `fetchSiblingNames` (at most 4 page GETs) and `fetchInvoicePdf` (checks `parent.data_source_id`, the Student relation and the Status, takes the first `type: "file"` entry, returns `{ number, fileUrl }` server-side only). Property-ID maps are cached 300 s in `lib/notion/cached.ts` (`getBillingPropertyIds`); billing rows are never cached.
- **Config** `lib/notion/config.ts`: `DEFAULT_INVOICES_DATA_SOURCE_ID`, `DEFAULT_CREDITS_DATA_SOURCE_ID`, `readBillingConfig()`, env overrides `NOTION_INVOICES_DATA_SOURCE_ID` and `NOTION_CREDITS_DATA_SOURCE_ID`, same `NOTION_TOKEN`.
- **Data** `apps/tracker/lib/data/parent-billing.ts` (`server-only`): `loadChildBilling(viewer, child, deps, today)` reads `students` (`notion_student_id, grade_level`) with the parent's own client, returns `not_available` for a null ID or config, runs child page, invoices and credits in parallel, then sharers and sibling names (fail soft), and returns `error` on any core throw. `invoicePdfResponse(params, deps)` is the whole AC9 and AC10 logic with injected dependencies. The file fetch uses `redirect: "error"`, a 15 s timeout and a 10 MB cap counted while reading; the filename is sanitised to `[A-Za-z0-9-]`.
- **View** `apps/tracker/lib/view/parent-billing.ts`: pure `buildBillingView` with strings already formatted (`$420`, `$62.50`, `-$60`, `Oct 3`, `October`, `Sep 2026`, `1.0h`). Rows carry `pdfHref` instead of any URL.
- **Page** replaces the placeholder; returns early for an unpublished child; server component only. **Route** `app/parent/[studentId]/billing/invoices/[invoiceId]/route.ts`, a thin GET wrapper, `force-dynamic`. **Component** `components/parent-billing.tsx` (summary card, Credits, Past invoices, rate line; every pill carries `data-state`).
- **Guardrail scan**: `BILLING_FORBIDDEN` and `PARENT_BILLING_FILES` in `apps/tracker/test/parent-guardrails.ts`; `apps/tracker/lib/parent-billing-scan.test.ts`.
- **Docs**: the ADR, the README index, PLAN.md progress.

## Migration
None. The parent already reads `tracker.students.notion_student_id` and `grade_level` under the 0016 policy "parent reads linked students". Invoices and credits stay in Notion; the tracker never writes them.

## Decisions (defaults accepted)
1. No new dependencies. 2. No migration. 3. "Credit applied" credits: Applied and named by `Applied To` or in the `Credits` relation; the amount is always the invoice's own Credit Applied (CAD). 4. Credit amount is Hours × Rate in the app. 5. Only displayed invoice fields plus Student and Credits are requested. 6. PDF streamed through the tracker with no-store; hosts `*.amazonaws.com`, `*.notion.so`, `*.notion-static.com`, plus the `NOTION_API_BASE` origin only when set; `external` refused. 7. A "View PDF" link on the card too. 8. Shared line on the card and past rows. 9. The PR states REQ-26 must be live first (it is: merged and applied 2026-10-16). 10. Pill tones as listed. 11. No caching of billing rows; property-ID maps 300 s. 12. Last 24 past invoices. 13. Name and `Grade N` header. 14. "Billing not available yet" and "Could not load billing. Try again later." 15. `BILLING_FORBIDDEN` in the shared guardrails file. 16. ADR number: next free at build time. 17. PDF opens in the same tab.
