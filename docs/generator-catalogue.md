4. Add them up: -8 + -24 + -12 + -2 = -46. So the remainder is -46.
5. Notice what that tells you: the remainder is not zero, so (x + 2) is not a factor of f(x). If it had come out to zero, it would have been.

**Sample questions**

<details>
<summary>Seed 0</summary>

**For which values of x is (x + 6)(x + 4)(x + 3) < 0? State the solution in interval notation.**

- **A.** `(-\infty, -6) \cup (-4, -3)` ← **correct**
- **B.** `(-6, -3)` _(treated_cubic_like_a_quadratic)_
- **C.** `(-6, -4) \cup (-3, \infty)` _(solved_for_the_opposite_sign)_
- **D.** `(-\infty, -6] \cup [-4, -3]` _(wrong_bracket_type_on_endpoints)_

Solution:

1. An inequality like this is a sign question, not a solving question. The factors are already there, so the first job is to find where the whole product can change sign — and that only happens where a factor is zero, at x = -6, x = -4 and x = -3.
2. Those three values cut the number line into four stretches: below -6, between -6 and -4, between -4 and -3, and above -3. Inside each stretch the sign of (x + 6)(x + 4)(x + 3) cannot change, so testing one convenient number in each is enough.
3. Doing that gives negative, then positive, then negative, then positive, reading left to right. That alternation is what you should expect: each factor is simple, so the product flips sign at every root.
4. You want where the product is negative, so take the first and third stretches.
5. Last, decide the brackets. The inequality is strict, so the roots make the product exactly zero and are excluded, which means round brackets. Either way infinity always gets a round bracket, because you never reach it.
6. So the solution is (-\infty, -6) \cup (-4, -3).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Seed 0</summary>

**Without solving it, determine the product of the roots of -3x^3 - 5x^2 - 7x - 1 = 0.**

- **C.** `\frac{1}{3}` _(dropped_the_negation_in_the_root_relation)_
- **D.** `1` _(ignored_the_leading_coefficient)_

Solution:

1. You are not being asked to solve anything, and you should not try. The roots are hiding in the coefficients already, because -3x^3 - 5x^2 - 7x - 1 is the expanded form of -3(x - r_1)(x - r_2)(x - r_3).
2. Multiply that out and match terms. The x^2 coefficient comes out as --3(r_1 + r_2 + r_3), and the constant term as --3r_1r_2r_3. So the sum of the roots is -b/a and the product is -d/a.
3. Here you want the product, so take the constant term, which is -1, put it over the leading coefficient -3, and negate.
4. That gives product = -(-1)/(-3) = -\frac{1}{3}.
5. Two things to keep straight: the minus sign is part of the relation and not optional, and you divide by the leading coefficient even when it is not 1. Skip either and you land on one of the other options here.
